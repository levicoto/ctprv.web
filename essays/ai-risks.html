The Risks of Artificial IntelligenceLos riesgos de la inteligencia artificial

Ignoring the risks of artificial intelligence is completely irresponsible. It is an irresponsibility toward your projects, your team, and society.

This is not about being alarmists, which is why they are called "risks," nor is it a call to idealize the future in a positive way; it is about understanding what we are facing and why it is worth continuing to develop artificial intelligence.

Not all the necessary decisions have been made to determine the future that awaits us.

It is everyone's responsibility to get involved and to make those decisions with clarity.

Ignorar los riesgos de la inteligencia artificial es totalmente irresponsable. Es una irresponsabilidad con tus proyectos, con tu equipo y con la sociedad.

No se trata de ser alarmistas, por eso se llaman "riesgos", tampoco es un llamado a idealizar el futuro de forma positiva; se trata de entender a qué nos enfrentamos y por qué vale la pena seguir desarrollando la inteligencia artificial.

No se han tomado todas las decisiones necesarias para determinar el futuro que nos espera.

Es responsabilidad de todos involucrarnos y tomar esas decisiones con claridad.
